Privacy Policy

Daily Ledger · Last updated: November 22, 2026

What is stored

  • On your device: your entries, categories, budgets and preferences are stored in your browser or app on that device. Without an account, nothing leaves your device.
  • If you create an account: your email address, a hashed password, and a copy of your entries (amounts, notes, categories, dates) and settings are stored on servers provided by Supabase so they can sync between your devices.

Who can see it

Your data is separated from other users by access rules, so other users cannot read it. Data is not end-to-end encrypted: the person who runs this app and the hosting provider can technically access stored data. Andrei Almacen will not read your entries except where needed to fix a problem you ask about or where required by law. Do not put information in notes that you would not want stored on a server.

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking or analytics scripts, and no selling or sharing of your data.

Email

Your email is used only for sign-in, confirming your account and resetting your password.

Your choices

  • Export your entries any time from Settings.
  • Delete your account any time from Settings > Sync > Delete my account. This permanently erases your server copy right away.
  • Signing out keeps the entries on that device. Clearing the browser's site data removes them.

Service providers

Supabase (database, sign-in and sending account emails) and Netlify (serving the app). Their servers may be outside your country.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change.
